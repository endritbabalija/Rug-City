# Game Design v2

Oct 11, 2026 · @endrit

The game grows from a string of separate cases into a detective career in the crypto underworld: 4 modes, 6 districts and 5 progression systems, built in four phases so each one is tested before the next is built.

## The fantasy and the world

You are a rookie on-chain detective who builds an agency, cracks cases across crypto's shadiest neighborhoods, and slowly closes in on the mastermind behind them all.

- **The Cryptoverse:** a city made of crypto niches. Each district has its own crimes, its own crime boss, and its own detective skill to master.
- **The Architect:** the mastermind behind every district boss. Each season reveals more of who he is, which gives the game a long-running story.
- **Your AI partner:** Detective Dex, a sarcastic, slightly broke detective who has seen every rug. One fixed partner at launch, with more partners later as unlockables. Partners you on solo cases, gives hints, cracks jokes, and remembers your past cases.
- **Recurring villains:** some escape and come back later, smarter and angrier. Players remember them and want revenge.
- **Everything is fictional parody:** no real people, projects or wallets, ever.

**Name:** Rug City. "Who rugged?" stays as the hook line and the name of the Daily.

## How it fits together

The game runs on one loop: every mode earns rewards, rewards unlock new districts and tools, and those make the next cases deeper.

&#91;embedded content: core loop · play, earn, grow\]

This loop is what the first version lacked: before, solving a case gave you nothing that carried into the next one.

**Text version of the loop**

- **Play:** Cases (solo with Dex or with a crew), Daily puzzle, The Mole, Weekly Bounty.
- **Earn:** Clue Points (permanent), Season Rating (resets), informant tips, caught villains.
- **Grow:** rank unlocks districts, HQ rooms unlock tools, the season pays TON prizes, the Wanted Board fills up.
- **Back to Play:** new districts and tools open new, harder cases.

## Modes

Four modes, each a different kind of fun, all built on one case engine. A fifth, Case Creator, comes once there are fans who want to make cases.

| Mode | Players | Length | What it is | Built on lessons from |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Cases** | Solo with the AI partner, or 2–6 with your crew | 15–20 min | Story cases inside a district. Clues split across players; empty seats filled by AI detectives | Werewolf for Telegram (group + private clues), Discord Werewolf (AI fills seats) |
| **Daily** | Solo | 2–3 min | One sharp puzzle, the same for everyone: "these 5 transactions, which is the insider?" Streaks and a spoiler-free share card | Wordle |
| **The Mole** | 4–8 friends | 10–15 min | One player is secretly the rugger and plants fake evidence; the rest must catch them. Sometimes the Mole is secretly an AI | Among Us, Werewolf, "Who's the Bot?" |
| **Weekly Bounty** | Everyone at once | 20 min, one window a week | A topical case on that week's crypto drama, sponsored, with a TON prize pool for the fastest correct players | Notcoin and Catizen (selling attention), HQ Trivia (what not to do with prizes) |
| **Case Creator** (later) | Creators | — | Players write cases, AI checks they are solvable, and creators earn a share of Stars sales | AI Dungeon (player-driven content) |

**The Mole guardrail:** the rugger plants misleading clues inside the game's fiction only. The game never teaches how to run a real scam.

## Districts

Six districts unlock in order as your rank rises. Each one introduces a new detective skill, so the game keeps changing how you play instead of just adding more of the same.

| # | District | Crimes | Skill it tests | District boss (working name) |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Memecoin Swamp | Rugs, pump-and-dumps | Follow the money from wallet to wallet | The Frog King |
| 2 | Sybil Square | Bot farms, wallet drainers, voting takeovers | Link many fake wallets to one person | The Puppeteer |
| 3 | DeFi Docks | Exploits, flash-loan attacks, hidden backdoors | Read simplified code and find the backdoor | The Engineer |
| 4 | NFT Gallery | Wash trading, stolen and copied art | Trace ownership: who sold to whom, which item is a copy | The Curator |
| 5 | Exchange Tower | Insider listings, missing customer funds | Spot what doesn't add up between the books and the chain | The Chairman |
| 6 | The Laundromat | Money hidden across chains and mixers | Trace funds across chains and mixers (endgame) | The Launderer |

- **Unlocking:** each district opens at a Clue Points rank. Paying players can get early access to a new district for one week (the Jackbox pack model).
- **Boss cases:** each district ends with a multi-part boss case. Catching the boss reveals a clue about The Architect.
- **Sponsorship:** a district can carry a partner's anti-scam theme, such as a wallet sponsoring Sybil Square.

## Progression

Five systems, each with one job: a permanent score, a seasonal score, a base to build, a reason to come back, and a collection to complete.

| System | What it does | How you earn it | Built on lessons from |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Clue Points → Rank** | Permanent score. Sets your rank (Rookie → Inspector → Chief → Legend) and unlocks districts. Never resets, and carries no promise of a token | Solving cases, accuracy, speed, streaks, Bounty results; a capped boost from purchases | Catizen and Notcoin (points), Hamster Kombat (no promises) |
| **Season Rating** | Resets every season (4 weeks for the first two seasons, then 8). Your best 20 cases of the season decide your share of the TON prize pool | Accuracy and speed in solo or crew cases, scored against each case's median time; any help, free or bought, lowers the score; it can't be bought | Pixels (rewards below revenue), HQ Trivia (reward the best, not everyone) |
| **Agency HQ** | Your base. Each room unlocks a tool: lab = Contract Scanner, server room = Chain Tracer, interview room = Lie Detector | Clue Points milestones, with Stars to speed up builds | Catizen (visible progress) |
| **Informants** | Idle loop. Hired informants collect tips while you're away; you spend tips as hints | Time away, plus more informants as you rank up | Catizen (come back to collect) |
| **Wanted Board** | Mugshot collection of every villain you catch. Rare villains can later become tradeable collectibles on TON | Catching culprits in Cases, Daily and Bounty | Collection games; option 2 collectibles |

## Rhythm

The game gives players a reason to open it every day, a big moment every week, and something new every season.

| When | What happens | Hook |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Every day | Daily puzzle, collect informant tips, one free Case (new players get 3 on day one) | Streak, tips waiting, "one more case" |
| Every week | Weekly Bounty on that week's crypto drama | Live event, TON prizes, bragging rights |
| Every week | New Cases added to unlocked districts | Fresh content |
| Every season (4 weeks, later 8) | New season: new district or story chapter, Season Rating resets, prize pool pays out, new season pass | A fresh start, a payout, a new story beat |
| A few times a year | A boss arc closes and a new Architect clue is revealed | Story cliffhangers |

## Monetization

Eight revenue lines, all tied to things players already want. Nothing sells a win, and every prize is paid from revenue.

| Revenue line | What players or partners pay for | Built on lessons from |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Season pass | Premium track: extra Cases, cosmetics, partner voices, bonus Clue Points | Mobile free-to-play standard |
| District early access | Play a new district one week before free players | Jackbox (paid packs) |
| Group unlocks | The host buys a case pack for the whole crew | Jackbox (one host pays, everyone plays) |
| Power-ups and boosts | Subpoena, Trace, extra interrogations, faster HQ builds, extra informants | Catizen (pay for speed), AI Dungeon (charge for costly AI) |
| Cosmetics | Detective outfits, HQ themes, AI partner voices (noir, degen, corporate) | Discord Werewolf (voice packs), Among Us (cosmetics) |
| Sponsors | Branded Weekly Bounty cases and sponsored districts with an anti-scam message | Notcoin and Catizen (selling attention) |
| Creator marketplace (later) | A cut of player-made case sales | AI Dungeon (player content) |
| Collectibles (later) | Royalties on Wanted Board villain cards traded on TON | Telegram collectible gifts |

**Prize rule:** 20% of net Stars revenue funds the season's TON pool; sponsors fund the Weekly Bounty pool. Prizes never come from your own pocket (the HQ Trivia lesson).

## Social and growth

Growth is built into play: group modes need friends, results are made to be shared, and Telegram groups become agencies.

- **Agencies (clans):** a Telegram group becomes an agency, with shared HQ upgrades, agency rankings and agency teams in the Weekly Bounty.
- **Invites that matter:** Cases and The Mole are better with friends, so the host invites the crew. Both get Clue Points once a new player finishes their first case.
- **Spoiler-free share cards:** a Wordle-style grid for Daily, and a verdict card for Cases.
- **Clip-ready moments:** a "caught red-handed" replay at the end of The Mole, built to be shared.
- **Channel partners:** crypto Telegram channels earn a share of revenue from players they send, through Telegram's affiliate program.
- **Topical marketing:** the Weekly Bounty rides each week's crypto drama, which gives free content to post.
- **Paid growth:** Stars earned get reinvested into Telegram ads with Telegram's 30% bonus.

## Guardrails

Leaderboards, prize pools and points attract cheaters fast, so anti-cheat ships with the first leaderboard, not after it.

| Area | Rule |
| --- | --- |
| Anti-cheat | One account per Telegram ID, rate limits, impossible-solve-time checks, Clue Points only from finished cases, a daily soft cap on Clue Points across all modes, and decisive clues that come from live interrogation so a case can't simply be pasted into a chatbot |
| Fair prizes | Season Rating can't be bought; prizes go to the best, weighted by rank; payouts on time, every time |
| Money safety | Prizes funded only from revenue and sponsors; payouts admin-approved until volume justifies automation |
| Token | No token at launch; Clue Points carry no promise; any token only after the decision gate in the concept doc |
| Content | Fictional parody only: no real people, projects or wallets. Cases show how scams are caught, never how to run one |
| Platform | Stars for all digital items; anything on-chain runs on TON |
| Legal | Legal check before real TON payouts, since paid boosts plus cash prizes can count as gambling in some countries |
| AI cost | Cases pre-generated and reused; cheapest good-enough model for live chat; costly AI features are paid |

## Roadmap

Build in four phases and only move on when the previous phase passes its gate. Phase 1 is about 2–3 weeks of work and fixes the two biggest gaps: single-player play and a reason to keep coming back.

&#91;embedded content: roadmap · 4 phases, 3 gates\]

Each phase is small enough to hand to Claude Code as one goal, with the gates showing what to measure before starting the next.

**Text version of the roadmap**

| Phase | What ships | Gate before the next phase |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 · Core (about 2–3 weeks) | Secure the Rug City name, Daily puzzle, Clue Points and rank (solo cases rated), Detective Dex as solo partner and as one crew seat, Memecoin Swamp with 12 cases plus two preview cases, case pipeline v1, 3 free cases on day one, admin broadcast and in-app banner, streaks, anti-cheat, Rug City rename at launch | 15%+ of players still playing after 7 days |
| 2 · World | Sybil Square and DeFi Docks, Agency HQ and tools, informants, season pass, share cards, more AI detectives with their own personalities | 2%+ of players pay, the season pass sells, and a legal opinion on TON prizes is received |
| 3 · Compete | The Mole, agencies, Weekly Bounty, TON season pool | A sponsor signs for a Bounty Case |
| 4 · Platform | Districts 4–6, Case Creator, collectibles, sponsored districts | — |

The legal opinion sits at Gate 2 so it is in hand before any TON payout goes live in Phase 3.

## Decisions

- [x] **Name: Rug City.** The game is a city of crypto districts, so name and world match; short, memeable, works as a bot name. "Who rugged?" stays as the hook line and the Daily's name. Runner-up: Chain Noir. Before committing, secure the Telegram bot username and domain and check trademarks.
- [x] **AI partner: one fixed character at launch, Detective Dex.** Sarcastic, slightly broke, has seen every rug. Dex becomes the mascot; more partners arrive from Season 2 as unlockables or cosmetics.
- [x] **Free play: one free Case a day, plus 3 free on day one.** Daily, informant tips and the Weekly Bounty are always free. The day-one cases double as the tutorial.
- [x] **Season length: 4 weeks for the first two seasons, then 8.** Faster learning and an earlier first payout; move to 8 weeks once one district per season is reliable.
- [x] **Second district: Sybil Square.** Reuses the Memecoin Swamp wallet engine and suits players who know airdrop bot farms. DeFi Docks comes third.
- [ ] **Legal and company setup: decide before Phase 3.** No cash prizes in Phases 1–2. Before TON payouts, get a fixed-fee opinion from a gaming and crypto lawyer, including a comparison of company jurisdictions for a founder based in Kosovo. Not legal advice.

* [x] **Season 1 ends early, on Nov 2.** It started Oct 5 as 8 weeks, but no prizes are paid yet, so shortening it costs players nothing. Nov 2 lines up with the Phase 1 launch, so Season 2 starts fresh with solo play and the new scoring, and new solo players don't join a season that crew players have already led for a month. Season 1 is treated as a beta. Seasons 2 (Nov 2–30) and 3 (Nov 30–Dec 28) run 4 weeks; 8-week seasons start with Season 4 on Dec 28. Tell current players about the new end date now.
* [x] **Solo cases with Dex earn Clue Points and Season Rating.** Change the rating rule from 3+ seats to every finished case. Each case is scored against its own median solve time, separately for solo and crew, so neither has an edge. Every Dex hint or informant tip lowers that case's score.
* [x] **Existing cases stay playable as previews.** mooncat is the first Memecoin Swamp case. airdrop and audit stay as one preview case each for Sybil Square and DeFi Docks, shown as locked districts on the map with one playable teaser. No re-theme needed. Memecoin Swamp needs more cases for launch, because one free case a day plus three on day one uses content fast.
* [x] **Phase 1 includes the 3 free day-one cases and a light version of AI seats.** The free cases are the tutorial. For AI seats, Dex can fill one empty seat in a crew case, so two friends plus Dex can play. Several AI detectives with their own personalities come in Phase 2.
* [x] **The Rug City rename ships with Phase 1, once the name is secured.** Securing the bot username and domain is the first task of Phase 1. The new name launches with Season 2 on Nov 2 as one marketing moment. If the name can't be secured, fall back to Chain Noir.

- [x] **All help lowers the score the same way, and no help makes a case unrated.** Dex hints, informant tips and bought Subpoenas, Traces and Hints each carry a fixed penalty (starting values: Hint −10%, Trace −15%, Subpoena −25% of the case score, stacking). A clean, unaided solve always scores higher than an aided one, which is what "rating can't be bought" means. Making only paid help unrated would punish paying players and contradict the free-hint rule.
- [x] **Fixed target time until a case has 20 plays, then the real median.** Counted separately for solo and crew, so each mode switches at its own 20 plays. Target times come from your own playtests. Recalculate medians once a week, not live, and never rescore results already earned.
- [x] **12 Memecoin Swamp cases at launch, drafted by AI and reviewed by you.** Hand-writing 350-line cases does not scale: one free case a day plus three on day one means an active player needs about 30 cases in a 4-week season. Phase 1 adds case pipeline v1: the three existing cases become the template, AI drafts new cases in that format, a blind solver check rejects unsolvable ones, and you review each before release. Target 5 new cases a week after launch, always keeping two weeks of cases in reserve. Players who finish everything see when the next case drops.
- [x] **Announce the Nov 2 end now in the channel and with an in-app banner; build the admin broadcast in Phase 1.** The announcement can't wait for new tooling, so it goes out through the channel plus a countdown banner in the game. The broadcast tool still belongs in Phase 1, because streak reminders and the Season 2 launch message on Nov 2 need it.
